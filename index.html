The Legend

Since 1948.
One sauce.
Zero leaks.

Folks have been driving to East Palatka for our BBQ sauce for longer than most of them have been alive. Since 1948, this rich, smoky, tangy-sweet sauce has been the talk of Putnam County — slathered on, dipped in, and argued over at more cookouts than anyone can count.

And the recipe? That's one rumor that never gets out. It's been handed down by word of mouth only, never written where anyone could find it, and guarded closer than the bar's cash drawer. People have guessed. People have bribed. People have tried to sweet-talk the bartender after a few rounds. Nobody's cracked it yet.

Come taste the secret for yourself — just don't bother asking what's in it.
